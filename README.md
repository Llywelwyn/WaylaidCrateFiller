# Waylaid Crate Filler

Open with `/crates`. The window also pops up on its own when you have Waylaid Crates in your bags.

Each crate in your bags gets a row with every material that can fill it and how many you have. Click a material to fill the crate with it, so there's no more right-clicking the crate and hunting through your bags.

- Scroll the mouse wheel over the window to fill the next crate (the highlighted material). Turn this off with `/crates wheel`.
- There's also a "Fill next crate" key binding under Key Bindings > AddOns.
- It picks the smallest stack that covers the whole amount, so small stacks get used up first.

Each fill is still one click or one scroll tick, since the game needs a real key or mouse press for every item use. Doesn't work in combat.
